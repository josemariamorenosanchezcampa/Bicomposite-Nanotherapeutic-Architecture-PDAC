# Scientific scope and limitations

This is a computational research package. On the evidence contained in the supplied v7.1 archive, the pharmacodynamic workbook should not be represented as experimentally measured responses from 100 patient-derived organoids. MMP9 is absent from the 20 deposited Cluster 7 markers. Computational outputs are hypothesis-generating and do not establish therapeutic efficacy, clinical safety, clinical utility or patient-specific response.
